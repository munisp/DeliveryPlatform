Real-Time Inputs vs. Governed Policy

Real-Time (Per Quote)
  • Route distance
  • Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA)
  • Gross rider fare
Governed (Weekly/Monthly)
  • Fuel cost index
  • Maintenance cost index
  • Variable platform cost
  • Contribution target
Why? Prevents real-time "surge pricing" from silently cutting driver share or hiding opaque penalties.
