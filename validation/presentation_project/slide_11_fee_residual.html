Pick the fee only after protecting driver economics

1. Base Economics
E = Rider Quote − Pass-throughs
D = E − Platform Fee + Subsidy
2. Mandatory Constraints
D ≥ Driver Floor
CM ≥ Target Contribution
Fee ≤ Published Cap (15%)
Dispatch only when D ≥ driver floor, platform contribution meets its target, and the fee remains within the published cap.
The platform fee is calculated as a residual within the cap, rather than a fixed percentage entitlement that ignores driver costs.
If the equation fails, use a disclosed subsidy, reprice within limits, reduce pickup, batch/schedule, or decline the trip.
