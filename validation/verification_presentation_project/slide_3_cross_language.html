A single accountable case connects each stakeholder check

verification_case

Binds subject, jurisdiction, purpose, lifecycle, decision, and expiry into one authoritative record.

Dependencies like consent_receipt, evidence, processing_job, and provider_check are idempotent, bounded records.

Append-only evidence and decision events produce a defensible, immutable chronology of the verification process.

Withdrawing consent immediately moves a verified case to a suspended state, ensuring continuous compliance.
